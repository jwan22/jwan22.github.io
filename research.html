Research

I study how galaxies form and evolve. I combine JWST observations with simulations and statistical modeling to understand the smallest galaxies at cosmic noon and the bursty, variable ways galaxies build up their stars.

Projects

Dwarf satellites at cosmic noon

Using JWST counts of satellite galaxies at 1 < z < 3.5 to test models of how subhalos and their galaxies are stripped by tides.

Star-formation histories

Building physically motivated priors for how star formation varies over time, and measuring that variability in real galaxies.

Galaxy clusters & cosmology

Earlier work combining X-ray and Sunyaev–Zel’dovich observations of galaxy clusters to measure the Hubble constant.
